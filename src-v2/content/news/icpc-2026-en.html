Engineering College (E|C) Light Academy, based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, is proud to announce an outstanding achievement by its Software Engineering students at the 2026 ICPC Kyrgyzstan Regional Contest, held on October 31 – November 1, 2026.

Our team, EC_CompetitivePromptEngineers, secured 12th place among 50 participating teams, competing alongside students from some of the country's leading universities. Even more remarkably, our team members are just 16–18 years old, demonstrating that the journey toward becoming a skilled engineer can begin well before university.

Throughout the competition, the team successfully solved four programming problems, earning 12th place in the national standings.

Next stage

A Place in the Next Stage

Engineering College participated in the competition with three teams. We are especially proud that EC_CompetitivePromptEngineers secured a qualifying position for the next stage of the ICPC competition, the North Eurasia Regional Finals (NERC), with the Almaty site among the host locations.

This is the first time in Engineering College's history that our students have advanced beyond the national stage of the competition, earning recognition alongside teams from established universities across Kyrgyzstan.

Why it matters

More Than a Competition Result

This achievement represents much more than a position in a programming contest. It demonstrates the quality and effectiveness of our Software Engineering program and validates our commitment to practical, project-based learning, analytical thinking, and early professional development.

At Engineering College, we believe that students do not need to wait until university to begin their journey as engineers. With the right environment, mentorship, curriculum, and opportunities to practice, young people can develop the skills needed to compete at a high level while still in secondary education.

Our students have demonstrated that age is not a barrier to innovation, problem-solving, or technical excellence. They can stand alongside university students, take on challenging problems, and earn their place among the best.

This is just the beginning. The next destination is Almaty!

We congratulate Aytmyrza, Daniyar, and Arthur on this remarkable achievement and thank our Software Engineering faculty and mentors for their dedication and support.
